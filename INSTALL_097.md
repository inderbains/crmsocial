6. Check Email history and Compliance — lawyer email history. The latter remains available on Completed/Archived sale files. Provider acceptance is not inbox delivery. No messages are sent automatically on preview/approval.

Safety: internal or identity categories and suspicious filenames (including Commission Statement.pdf) are excluded. A genuine commission disclosure must be separately reviewed and stored under the correct category and filename. This is a conservative filter, not content inspection. Gmail does not offer provider idempotency; a database unique key prevents parallel duplicate sends and blocks repeat identical attempts with failed or uncertain status until manually reconciled. No automatic retry. Test provider errors, duplicates, unauthorized callers and separate brokerages. Live Supabase/email and full Next.js build are not verified here.
