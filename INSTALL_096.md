On a non-archived sale it lists its own documents and its actual parent listing's visible documents. This uses parent_listing_id, not address matching. Standalone sales have no linked history. Existing row-level policies determine visibility; no new privileges are granted. Related documents are read-only, kept separate from current-file compliance, commission and closing functions.
